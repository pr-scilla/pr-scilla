<h1 align="center">Hi! I'm Priscilla Pierce.</h1>

Currently, I'm a graduate student in the  [Master of Environmental Data Science](https://bren.ucsb.edu/masters-programs/master-environmental-data-science/academics-meds) (MEDS) program at the University of Santa Barbara Bren School of Environmental Science and Management. In my professional life, I have supported freshwater research ecology projects, assisted with city water conservation programs, analyzed groundwater extractions in critically overdrafted basins, and overseen invasive agricultural pest quarantine projects for the state of California. In my free time I love caring for my pets (including four spoiled chickens!), growing and preserving my own food, hiking, and spending time with my family.
